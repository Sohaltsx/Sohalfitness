IT Developer Health Tips
🪑
90-Min Desk Rule
Set a timer every 90 min. Stand up, stretch hip flexors and thoracic spine for 3 min. Sitting all day stiffens your hips and makes leg day much harder.
💧
Water Bottle at Desk
Keep a 1L bottle visible on desk. Refill twice. Dehydration = fatigue + poor code focus. Your body water (33.6 L) is already low.
👀
20-20-20 Eye Rule
Every 20 min, look 20 feet away for 20 seconds. Reduces eye strain — React debugging sessions are long.
🍵
No Tea After 4 PM
Caffeine after 4 PM delays sleep by 1–2 hours. Poor sleep raises cortisol → stores fat. Swap to green tea or chaach.
🚶
Walk During Calls
Standup meetings, 1-on-1s — walk during these. 15 min of walking burns ~60 kcal and clears mental fog.
📵
No Work After 9 PM
Late-night debugging spikes cortisol, destroys sleep quality, and directly increases belly fat. Log off and recover.
